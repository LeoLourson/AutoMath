# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can report a bug, propose a command, or question
an agent design without being insulted. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or the size of the contribution.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Giving a reproducible case instead of a verdict: the command, the versions, the output.
- Criticising the workflow, the code, or the documentation, not the person.
- Assuming the other side has a reason and asking for it before assigning blame.
- Accepting that a maintainer may reject a change for reasons of scope or workflow coherence.

Examples of behaviour that does not belong here:

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and dismissive comments about someone's skill.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through GitHub: open a discussion marked private, or use
**Security → Report a vulnerability** when the report itself must stay private. The maintainer
will review the report, may ask for details, and will answer within a week. Consequences range
from a warning to a permanent ban from the repository, depending on the severity and the pattern.
